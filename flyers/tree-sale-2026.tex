\sectionHeader{RECENT COMMUNITY INVOLVEMENT \& HIGHLIGHTS}

\highlightRow
  {Grilling Up Burgers \& Hot Dogs}
  {Serving hot dogs and burgers from our mammoth grill at the Memorial Day Parade, youth sports jamborees, Joppa Hill Educational Farm events, town employee luncheons, and Peter Woodbury School's 4th-grade graduation.}
  {Town Council \& School Board Forums}
  {Hosting annual February candidate forums---including the 2026 local elections---broadcast live and on-demand on BCTV so Bedford voters can hear directly from candidates on key community issues.}

\vspace{5pt}

\highlightRow
  {Hawkins Field Dugout Repair}
  {Rebuilt the Hawkins Field dugouts with durable, low-maintenance composite materials---supporting adult softball and youth soccer while honoring club founder and former NH State Rep.\ Ken Hawkins.}
  {Trunk or Treat}
  {Bringing club pride to Bedford's annual Trunk or Treat with the Bedford Men's Club banner, creative trunk decorations, and plenty of goodies for local families and children.}
